According to a TechRadar report published November 25, a US law firm is investigating whether three browser extensions linked to Urban VPN captured and shared users' AI chatbot conversations without consent.

:::warn
These are **allegations**. No court has ruled on them and no settlement exists. The firm is still reviewing claims from eligible users.
:::

## 1. Which extensions are named

The report names three Chrome and Edge extensions:

- Urban VPN Proxy
- 1ClickVPN Proxy
- Urban Browser Guard

More than **8 million users** may have been affected.

## 2. What is alleged

On July 9, 2025, version 5.5.0 of Urban VPN Proxy was pushed through browser auto-updates and allegedly added code that captured AI prompts. The data was reportedly sent to Urban VPN-linked analytics domains and allegedly passed to the data broker BiScience.

The most striking claim: collection reportedly **continued even when the VPN was switched off** or the extension's "AI Protection" toggle was disabled.

Platforms named include ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Microsoft Copilot, Perplexity, DeepSeek, Grok and Meta AI.

## 3. How it came to light

Security firm Koi Security published its findings in December 2025. The law firm's inquiry covers California residents who had one of the extensions installed between July 9 and December 15, 2025, and used an AI chatbot in the same browser.

## 4. Why it matters

Browser extensions can hold sweeping permissions, including reading the pages you visit. A free tool marketed for privacy can become the very thing that leaks your data, which is why we keep coming back to one question: who pays for a free service?

It also exposes a common misconception. A VPN does not hide what you type from the AI company. It only encrypts the connection between you and the VPN server.

## 5. What to do now

1. **Audit your extensions** and remove anything you don't recognize or no longer use
2. **Read permission prompts** before installing. "Read and change all your data on all websites" is the broadest access there is
3. **Be wary of free privacy tools** and favor services with a clear privacy policy and independent audits
4. **Keep sensitive details out of AI chats** regardless of what tools you use
