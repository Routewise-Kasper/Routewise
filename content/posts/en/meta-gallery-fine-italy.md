On November 14, Italy's data protection authority, the Garante, announced an €805 million fine (about $887 million) against Meta Platforms Ireland. It found that Facebook's Android app accessed photos, videos and related metadata on users' phones without specific, informed consent.

## What happened

The issue centers on a Facebook Android feature that suggests photos from your phone's gallery while you browse.

According to reports citing the decision, Facebook used a single Android permission to access the gallery for several features with different purposes. Users saw a generic prompt about photo access that didn't explain their photos would drive post suggestions. The regulator said that didn't amount to valid consent. The feature was also on by default.

## How the amount was set

The Garante said the fine is about 0.47% of the group's worldwide turnover. It cited roughly 20 million Android users in Italy as the scale of the practice. That figure reflects the affected population the regulator considered, not a confirmed count of galleries actually read. Reports say it also weighed a 2019 decision against Meta and what it called poor cooperation.

## What Meta must do

Meta has 60 days to turn the feature off by default, even for people who previously granted gallery access. To bring it back, it must ask for separate, specific consent that clearly explains the gallery access, its purpose and how to turn it off.

Meta reportedly called the fine disproportionate and plans to appeal.

## Why it matters to you

The case is Italian, but the lesson isn't: **one phone permission often serves more than the feature you granted it for.** Give an app photo access to set a profile picture, and technically any feature in that app can use it. Android and iOS have been moving toward finer-grained access, such as sharing only selected photos, for exactly this reason.

## What you can check now

On Android, go to Settings, Apps, pick the app, then Permissions and Photos and videos. On iPhone, go to Settings, Privacy & Security, Photos. Where you can, choose limited access to selected photos rather than everything, and turn off "suggestions from your gallery" style features you don't use.
