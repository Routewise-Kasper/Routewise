NodesNews is a plain-language guide to getting online.

Search for help with a network problem and you'll often find answers that are outdated or buried in jargon. Our goal is simple: **explain one thing clearly, from start to finish**, so anyone can follow along.

## What we cover

- **Basics**: what VPNs, split tunneling and servers actually are
- **How-to**: setting things up on different devices
- **Troubleshooting**: step-by-step fixes when things break
- **Privacy**: how to judge whether a service deserves your trust
- **Travel**: what to prepare before a trip

## Our principles

- We only write what can be verified. No hype, and no knocking other services
- Guides are reviewed regularly and show a last-updated date
- When we recommend something, we disclose it. See our [disclosure](/en/disclosure/)
