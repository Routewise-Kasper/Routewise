Some NodesNews articles contain affiliate links. If you buy a service through one of them, we may earn a commission, at no extra cost to you.

## What this means

- We have **commercial relationships** with some of the services we recommend
- Articles with affiliate links are clearly labeled at the top
- Affiliate links start with `/go/`, so they're easy to spot

## How we keep content trustworthy

- Tutorials and troubleshooting guides apply to any service
- We describe only product features we can verify, without superlatives like "fastest" or "best"
- We don't disparage other services
